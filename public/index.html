Questions, answered

why not?

Who can join the Innovation Center?+

Any currently enrolled student can join, whatever your department, year, or current level of experience.

Do I need a project idea first?+

Yes. You should have a project idea, question, or problem you want to explore. Bring your starting point and we will help you develop it.

Are events free?+

Yes. Workshops, talks, and community meetups are free for university students.

Why we exist

Campus is more than a place. It’s a launchpad.

We connect ambitious students with the tools, mentors, and community to make their first meaningful thing. No gatekeeping. No waiting for permission.
